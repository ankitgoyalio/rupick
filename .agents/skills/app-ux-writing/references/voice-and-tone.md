# Voice and tone

Use this exercise when defining a voice or when existing copy lacks a coherent personality. Reuse a documented voice for routine work.

## Define a usable voice

Describe what the app does and whom it serves in one sentence. Imagine it as a person and generate traits that fit that relationship. Group related traits into themes and select the qualities that best express its values. Keep an important outlier if it adds a useful distinction; the grouping is a thinking aid.

Translate each chosen quality into an observable writing choice and a short example. A label such as “friendly” alone leaves too much room for interpretation. For example, a supportive learning app might acknowledge effort, explain the next step directly, and give neutral recovery instructions after a mistake.

Try the voice on a welcome message. Start with the real benefit, choose a verb that fits the relationship, and add enough specificity to express what this app does for this audience. Compare the result with the initial generic sentence. The exercise is complete when the qualities produce distinct, useful wording that fits the product.

## Adapt tone to the moment

Voice is the enduring expression of the app's values; tone is its situational balance. Consider what happened, what the person may be feeling, and what they need next. Increase or reduce the prominence of the voice's qualities accordingly. Traits such as trustworthiness remain intact in every state.

| Situation | Emphasis | Writing consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First encounter | Orientation and welcome | Explain the relevant benefit and how to begin; allow room for a helpful example. |
| Routine action | Familiarity and efficiency | Use established terms and direct actions. |
| Earned accomplishment | Recognition and warmth | Name the actual achievement; a celebratory phrase or exclamation can fit. |
| Blocked task | Clarity and recovery | State the problem and a supported next step; retain details needed to fix it. |
| Sensitive or consequential event | Precision and calm | State the known facts and choices without judgment or forced cheerfulness. |

These priorities adapt to the app's own voice. They are not a requirement to use Apple's qualities or a fixed numerical scoring system.

## Resolve brevity and personality together

The 2024 talk allows extra words for purposeful warmth; the 2025 talk removes words that contribute no meaning. Apply both: decide whether a phrase contributes to the relationship at this moment. A congratulation may help someone feel recognized; a playful interjection in a failed upload may undermine the recovery message.

Evaluate adjectives, adverbs, apologies, pleasantries, and punctuation individually. Keep them when they convey necessary behavior or serve an appropriate tone. Avoid blanket deletion rules that flatten the voice or change meaning.

Keep vocabulary decisions in the applicable `WORD_LIST.md`, following the word-list guidance in the main skill.
